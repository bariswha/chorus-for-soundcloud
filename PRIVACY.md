# Privacy

Chorus has no backend, no analytics and no tracking. The authors never receive any of your data.

## What stays on your device

- Your keys, friend list, messages and settings, in `chrome.storage.local`.
- Your listening stats: which tracks you played, for how long, and when. They power the Stats page and your yearly recap. You can turn recording off, export your stats or delete them in **Settings → Stats**.

## What other people can see

| Who | What |
| --- | --- |
| People in your Jam | Your display name, avatar and SoundCloud profile link, the Jam queue, chat messages and reactions. |
| Your friends | Whether you're online, the track you're playing (you can turn this off in Settings), whether you're in a Jam, and your stats summary if you turn on **Let friends see my stats**. |
| Public relay servers | Only encrypted data, plus your IP address and connection times, like any server you connect to. |
| LRCLIB | The title, artist and duration of a track when you open its lyrics. |

## What Chorus does with SoundCloud

Chorus reads the track you're playing and your public SoundCloud name and avatar from the page you have open. It controls the player in that tab and asks SoundCloud for related tracks to make suggestions. It never sees your password or sends anything to SoundCloud on your behalf beyond what the SoundCloud page itself does.

## Deleting your data

To remove everything, uninstall the extension. That clears all local storage. You can delete your stats alone in **Settings → Stats → Clear stats**.
